Race your friends at 35,000 feet. No Wi-Fi needed.

Cabin Fever is a party game for when you're stuck on a plane with friends and there's no internet. Everyone flies their own plane on their own iPhone or iPad, connected straight to each other. Last plane standing wins.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • Offline multiplayerUp to 6 players over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. No signal, no accounts.
  • Three ways to raceIn the air, on the airport apron, and through the terminal maze.
  • Crash? Get revengeKnocked-out players become storm clouds and rain lightning on whoever's left.
  • Real turbulenceYour phone feels the plane's real bumps and shakes the game with them.
  • Tournaments3 to 10 rounds, points for every finish, a podium, and stakes of your choice.
  • Solo tooTake on bots, or fly Solo infinite and see how far you get.

Last plane standing

Dodge thunderheads, balloons and geese while a headwind pushes you back towards the storm. Crash, and you become the storm.

Power-ups and power-downs

Grab a shield, shove rivals with a bumper bubble, hide behind smoke, or jam everyone with a rare EMP. Steer clear of Jumbo and Upside down.

Then race on the ground

Drive baggage tractors and pushback tugs across the apron, dodge taxiing airliners, and outrun the police.

And through the terminal

Find your way to the gate before the final-call shutters close behind you.

Or fly solo, forever

One plane, endless sky, faster and faster. Beat your best and climb the Game Center leaderboard.

Free to play. Captains host the big games.

Solo play and joining friends' games are free, and anyone can host a quick game for two over three rounds.

The one-off Captain's Licence lets you host up to 6 players and tournaments of any length, and adds the captain's hat, pilot's wings and a gold money trail. No subscriptions, no adverts.
