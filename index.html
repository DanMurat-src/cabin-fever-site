Race your friends, wherever you are. No Wi-Fi needed.

Cabin Fever is a party game for whenever you're together: stuck on a plane, on the train, in the back of the car, waiting for the bus, or just round the table. Everyone flies their own plane on their own iPhone or iPad, connected straight to each other with no internet. Last plane standing wins.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • Offline multiplayerUp to 6 players over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. Works at 35,000 feet, in a tunnel, or anywhere without a signal. No accounts.
  • Three ways to raceIn the air, on the airport apron, and through the terminal maze.
  • Crash? Get revengeKnocked-out players become storm clouds and rain lightning on whoever's left.
  • Wildcard roundsSome rounds throw in a twist: fog, a tailwind, jumbo jets, upside-down flying or a lightning storm.
  • Taunt your friendsSend a quick 😂, 😎 or GG over your plane mid-race, without getting in anyone's way.
  • Real turbulenceYour phone feels real bumps, on a plane, a train or a bumpy road, and shakes the game with them.
  • Tournaments3 to 10 rounds, points for every finish, stakes of your choice, and double points in the final round, so nobody's out of it.
  • Target on the leaderWhoever leads on points flies with a crosshair on them, and the storms strike faster on the leader. Stay on top if you can.
  • Save the podiumFinish a tournament and save a picture of the podium, with everyone's planes, names and the stakes, to post wherever you like.
  • Make it yoursUnlock accessories, paint jobs and jet streams, and mix and match them. Your friends see them on every screen.
  • Daily flightA new course every day, the same for everyone in the world. Keep your streak going and top today's leaderboard.
  • Solo tooFly an infinite flight to see how far you get, or take on up to five bots in a tournament.

Last plane standing

Dodge thunderheads, balloons and geese while a headwind pushes you back towards the storm. Crash, and you become the storm.

Power-ups and power-downs

Grab a shield, shove rivals with a bumper bubble, hide behind smoke, or jam everyone with a rare EMP. Steer clear of Jumbo and Upside down.

Then race on the ground

Drive baggage tractors and pushback tugs across the apron, dodge taxiing airliners, and outrun the police.

And through the terminal

Find your way to the gate before the final-call shutters close behind you.

Or fly solo, forever

One plane, endless sky, faster and faster. Beat your best, fly the daily flight, and climb the Game Center leaderboards.

Free to play. Captains host the big games.

Solo play and joining friends' games are free, and anyone can host a quick game for two over three rounds.

The one-off Captain's Licence lets you host up to 6 players and tournaments of any length, and adds the captain's hat, pilot's wings, a gold money trail and the Money paint job: gloss black covered in gold £ $ €. No subscriptions, no adverts.

Game Center ready

Cabin Fever works with Apple's Game Center. Compare your furthest Solo flight with friends and the world, race for the top of today's Daily flight leaderboard, and collect 71 achievements, from your first flight to Pilot of the Day.

No signal on the plane? No problem. Everything you earn offline is kept on your device and goes up to Game Center the next time you're connected.

Achievements also unlock 31 accessories for your plane, from a football helmet to a superhero cape, each with its own jet stream to fly behind you, 16 paint jobs from racing stripes to chrome, and a mystery reward for earning every one.
